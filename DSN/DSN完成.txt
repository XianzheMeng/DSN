This manuscript proposes DSN, a hybrid neural-network framework that fuses untrained Spiking Neural Networks (SNN) with Hyper-dimensional Computing (HDC) for ultra-low-power EMG classification.
It eliminates back-propagation by using random SNN weights for feature extraction and one-pass HDC encoding for classification, achieving 42× lower energy than GRU while maintaining >93% accuracy.
The work provides formal robustness guarantees (Lipschitz bounds, noise resilience, bit-flip fault tolerance) and demonstrates theoretical and practical advantages for edge-deployable neural networks.

By eliminating back-propagation and reducing energy 42-fold versus GRU while keeping >93% accuracy, DSN offers the first rigorously guaranteed, training-light neural-network solution for wearable EMG monitoring. This breakthrough lowers the deployment barrier for real-time, battery-powered edge healthcare devices and establishes a template for similarly efficient, robust neural networks in other mobile or implantable applications.
